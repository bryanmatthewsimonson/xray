## 2026-10-01 — An empty archive over 158 "confirmed" publishes: relay.primal.net keeps events for days, and the portal now reads the case's own publish record

**Tags:** bug, relays, portal, identity

**Report.** The recaptured COVID case's archive listed nothing: "Local
ledger says 158 published; the relays confirm 0; 158 missing". Every
publish had gone to `wss://relay.primal.net` only, signed by the case's
bound identity.

**Cause (measured 2026-10-01, read-only REQs from a session).**
relay.primal.net answers `OK true` and does not keep what it accepted:
- long-form (30023): about 1,100 held in all, 98% from the three days
  before the query (09-29: 37, 09-30: 684, 10-01: 361);
- 30040, 30041, 30054, 30055, 32125 and 1985: nothing older than about
  36 hours, and not one event in a kind only X-Ray emits (32125, 30055,
  30068, 30069, 30070), from any author;
- control: two prolific authors' kind-1 history is 2 notes on primal and
  500+ on nostr.oxtr.dev, back a year. nos.lol behaves like primal.

The publish ledger counts a CONFIRMED relay `OK` (JOURNAL 2026-07-10).
That is acceptance, not storage, so the ledger said "published" and the
portal, the first thing that reads back, was right to say "missing".
The July COVID corpus, published to six relays, is still on the ones
that keep events: nostr.oxtr.dev (2,416 of its events), relay.nostr.net
(1,974), offchain.pub (995), nostr.mom (101, articles only); primal and
nos.lol hold none of it. Size limits: 128 KB at oxtr, offchain and mom,
256 KB at relay.nostr.net, 1 MB at relay.damus.io and primal, so a long
transcript fits only the last three. The runbook's relay probe exists for
exactly this (EPISTACK_RUNBOOK §1, `tools/relay-probe.mjs --recheck`
marks accepted-but-purged as a reject); its Results section was never
filled in.

**Recovery.** The signed events are in the case's journal. Add relays
that keep events in Settings ▸ Relays, then Archive ▸ "Missing from the
relays" ▸ "Rebroadcast all missing" re-sends them verbatim, and Refresh.

**The first diagnosis was wrong, and how.** It took an exported brief's
header (the July corpus's key and six relays) for this case's and blamed
the portal's key. It also measured "primal holds nothing under either
key" and read that as a settings mistake, not as the contradiction it
was: a relay that confirmed every publish and holds none of them. The
measurement that settled it was retention.

**What changed (the three the maintainer asked for).**
1. `portal/identity.js` gains two "me" sources. `case-identity` is the
   active workspace's bound identity. `journal` is a saved identity
   PROFILE that signed an operator-kind event in this case's `xray-events`
   journal (`portal/publish-history.js`). Only profiles: merge-import
   carries a collaborator's journal rows in verbatim, and their key must
   not become "me" (the 28.4 fence). Entity kind-0 and kind-1 rows never
   count. `resolverIdentity` (`portal/audit-data.js`) moved from a
   denylist to an allowlist (`signer`, `publish-history`, `manual`): the
   new sources are local keys, never evidence of a NIP-07 or bunker
   signer, so they must not mint a resolution coordinate.
2. The portal reads from the configured relays plus up to eight relays
   that CONFIRMED events signed in this case by a key this install holds
   or chose: the signer, the sync key, the bound identity, a saved
   profile. Never a key that is "me" only through a claim's publish stamp,
   which merge-import can carry in. wss, or ws on a loopback host; an
   assumed send is no evidence. Publishing and rebroadcast stay on the
   configured list. The footer names every relay read, and the sync
   cursor is keyed on the read set.
3. `shared/case-membership.js` compares the live Local signer with the
   case's bound identity (`signerBindingState`, `describeSignerBinding`)
   and words a mismatch once (`signerBindingWarning`). NIP-07 and
   NSecBunker are never compared: under Option C they may differ. The
   portal shows a banner; the reader's case chip turns amber and Publish
   asks first. The drift paths: Settings ▸ Signing "Use", a full backup
   restore (it replaces `local_primary_identity` and `preferences`, not
   the workspace pointer), and a rebind.

None of the three would have shown this case's corpus: no relay the
portal can ask holds it.

**Not changed (follow-ups).**
- Reconcile cannot tell confirmed-then-dropped from rejected. The
  journal records which relay confirmed; a read-back some hours after
  publish, per relay (the probe's recheck), would name the relay that
  dropped it.
- `nostr-client.js` ignores `CLOSED` and `NOTICE`. A relay that refuses a
  REQ is waited out to the timeout and reported as answered-with-nothing,
  not failed: primal answered an oversized `#r` filter with
  `NOTICE "ERROR: bad req: filter item too large"` and no EOSE.
