## Verdict

The governance corpus is well-made and wrongly sized. Five normative documents (2,804 lines: CONSTITUTION 619, PHILOSOPHY 269, TRUTH_SYSTEMS 727, DISCIPLINES 630, TRUTH_INFRASTRUCTURE 559) plus a 362-line founding transcript plus ~30 design docs plus 216 lines of wayfinding in CLAUDE.md sit over a codebase whose actual, maintainer-ratified doctrine fits on one page. Of the 22 distinct enforcement mechanisms in `src/` and `tests/` that call themselves a firewall (census in §Findings F0), **seven** protect something the maintainer demonstrably decided (grounding, human-accept, the membrane, no auto person-label, argument-not-conclusion, no intent, import consent) and are excellent; **eight** are agent generalizations of those seven, graded E5 by the project's own questionnaire, that now block capabilities the maintainer has said he wants (aggregates as instruments, a combined reader view, a durable lens cache, an entity-level summary); and **seven** are prose pins — regexes over identifier names, banned-word lists over rendered strings, verbatim quotes of agent-drafted clauses — that observe no behavior at all and convert every reword into a Tier-1 ceremony. The maintainer's own words are the yardstick: "no aggregations was never the plan" (2026-07-21, JOURNAL:3527), and the constitution itself agrees — "refusing them wholesale was itself a form of false precision" (CONSTITUTION.md:253) — yet `corpus-rollup.js:4-6` still refuses a mean while permitting a range, `corpus-publish.test.mjs:83` bans any percentage from a case brief, and the entity-record ratio that TRUTH_ADJUDICATION §3.5 explicitly licenses (TRUTH_ADJUDICATION_DESIGN.md:312-317) has never been built because `entity-dossier.test.mjs:185` bans the word "credibility" from the object's keys.

The root defect is not any single rule. It is that the corpus has **no way to tell a maintainer ruling from an agent's plausible reading**: 11 maintainer-decision markers in a 10,823-line JOURNAL, at most two per design doc, zero in the constitution's articles, and the guard suite pins the text regardless (Q12). So the reconciliation the maintainer announced on 2026-08-28 currently requires an excavation (PR #366 is 667 lines of it) rather than a grep. That is the thing to fix first, because every future rule will otherwise re-accrete the same way. Three governance PRs in the last week (#364 +354, #365 +528, #366 +667 — 1,549 lines about governance, zero lines of product) are the symptom: the system now spends its scarcest resource — maintainer attention — on reading law about itself.

The fresh-eyes answer: keep the seven real firewalls as law, restate Article 6 as one sentence, define the one word the whole regime turns on ("fused") so eleven hand-rolled banned-word lists can become one schema check, demote every prose pin to either a structural drift-detector or nothing, add a literal `[INTERPRETATION]` / `[RULING]` marker protocol with a grep-able `docs/RULINGS.md`, and run the reconciliation as **one 90-minute session with six decisions** (agenda in §Fresh-start design). Governance humility note: every disposition below that touches an E4/E5 rule is a *recommended default*, phrased as a question in the last section; "keep unchanged" is a complete answer to each.

## What is good (KEEP)

- **The seven real firewalls are real, cheap, and behavioral** — grounding-as-search-key (`llm-proposals.js:164-200`, `quote-grounding.js`; JOURNAL 2026-07-03 E3), per-row human accept (`llm-proposals.js:9-14`, `sidepanel/index.js:1991`), the truth-adjudicability gate at build/parse/create/UI (`truth-taxonomy.js:397-424`, `truth-builders.js:258-260, 369`, `truth-adjudication-model.js:542-545`, `adjudicate-modal.js:288-291`), the value firewall (`integrity-model.js:19-21`), no auto person-label (`truth-entity-record.js:220-235`), argument-never-conclusion in the opinion prompt (`audit/module-prompts.js:855`, transcript Exchange 1 — the one rule with a founding antecedent), and the import-consent/verify path (`extraction-import.js`, MA.7; `mergeBackup` local-wins). Each blocks a concrete harm to a real user and each is enforced at a choke point, not in prose. This is what "invariants are enforced, not commented" (architect S7) looks like when done right.
- **The membrane** — accept-only incorporation, viewing writes nothing (`incorporation.js:19`, NETWORK_CLIENT_DESIGN header). E1, recorded in the maintainer's voice, and the model for how a design doc should carry its decisions.
- **The MA.6 whole-unit disclosure reversal** (JOURNAL:3220-3290) — the maintainer's own ruling, recorded with its reasoning, and the one place the corpus shows a rule being *reversed* for being over-cautious. It is the precedent this whole lens argues from.
- **Art. 3 (exposure never deletion), Art. 7's reverse-criticism derivation, Art. 8's gateless accountability, Art. 10's reserved-vs-retired passage, and the Preamble's cult self-check.** These are the constitution's genuine content; Art. 8 and Art. 9 are E1 (maintainer revised them pre-ratification, JOURNAL:2436-2511). Nothing here should be touched.
- **Art. 11 kill-and-revisit and the 2026-07-21 JOURNAL entry** — "check which kind a recorded decision was before treating it as a constraint" is exactly the discipline the corpus then failed to institutionalize. The lesson is right; it just never became a marker.
- **The guard idiom** (positive sanity assertion, then the negative assertion) and the *structural* pins: heading order, citations resolve, version stamps agree (`constitution-guards.test.mjs:61-92, 121-151, 178-195`). Cheap drift detectors with no doctrinal content — keep.
- **MARGIN_DESIGN §10's constraint-provenance table** (MARGIN_DESIGN.md:327-345) with Constitutional / Statute / Maintainer ruling / Engineering fact / Process kinds and `[R]` flags. This is the seed of the marker protocol; it should be the template, not a one-off.
- **The 2026-08-04 "merge authority waived, on the record" entry** (JOURNAL:2242-2270) — a rule was broken, and the record says so instead of pretending. Exactly Art. 4.5 applied to governance.
- **PR #366's evidence ladder and inventory.** It is the best single artifact on this topic in the repo; its self-imposed "no recommendations" is its only flaw for the session's purposes.

## Fresh-start design

If I started X-Ray's governance today for a solo maintainer directing agents toward a tool for non-technical research groups, I would build a system whose first property is that **a human decision is a first-class, grep-able object**, and whose second property is that **law is one page**.

Net: fewer tests, every one of which observes something.

### (b) The decision-confirmation protocol

1. **Literal markers, three, everywhere a constraint is stated** (design docs, kickoffs, PR bodies, guard headers, prompt files): `[RULING: maintainer YYYY-MM-DD R-id]`, `[INTERPRETATION: <agent>, YYYY-MM-DD — default: <x>; ask: <one line>]`, `[ENGINEERING-FACT: <measured how>]`. A constraint with no marker is an INTERPRETATION by definition (Q18's "everything unmarked is drafting").
2. **PR template section "Interpretive steps (n)"** — required, may be "0". Each step is one line with its recommended default. A PR whose interpretive steps are not listed is not soak-ready; `hand-to-maintainer` carries them to the maintainer as questions, never inside the diff's prose.
3. **Rulings are recorded once, in RULINGS.md, at merge** — the maintainer's own words quoted (≤ 2 lines), the R-id, what it applies to. JOURNAL entries cite the R-id rather than restating. A guard test asserts every `R-` id cited anywhere in the tree exists in RULINGS.md (the P-citation guard generalized).
4. **Guard provenance and expiry** — every `tests/*guards*.mjs` test and every doc-pinning assertion carries `// Provenance: R-… ` or `// Provenance: INTERPRETATION (<date>) — expires <date+90d>`. The meta-guard fails an expired INTERPRETATION guard with "renew (get an R-id) or remove". Interpretations therefore cannot silently become law by aging.
5. **Asking the maintainer** — one question per message, in this exact shape: *Question* (one sentence) / *Current rule and its provenance* (grade + cite) / *Options* (≤ 3, each with its cost in one line) / *Recommended default* / *"Reply `keep` to keep unchanged — that is a complete answer."* Questions are batched into a single decision only when they are logically dependent (the D1–D6 batching below is the model). Silence is not consent: an unanswered question stays an INTERPRETATION with its expiry.
6. **Amendment ceremony scaled to provenance, not just tier** — confirming an existing INTERPRETATION into a RULING is a one-line RULINGS.md row plus the marker flip; it does not need Tier-1 artifacts, because nothing changes but the label. Tier-1 ceremony is reserved for changing what the text *says*.
